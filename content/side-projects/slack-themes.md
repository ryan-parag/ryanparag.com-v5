For anyone juggling five or more Slack workspaces, the "wrong window" mistake is a constant risk. I created this gallery to help users quickly distinguish between their clients, side projects, and primary jobs. It’s a curated collection of copy-pasteable sidebar themes that range from professional to vibrant, designed to help users organize their digital workspace with better visual hierarchy.
