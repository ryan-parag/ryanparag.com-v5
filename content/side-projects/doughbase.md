Doughbase started in my own kitchen. I'd been working my way through pizza recipes I could make at home, from New York-style to Neapolitan, and kept flipping between sites to figure out which dough I wanted to make that week (and how much time it would take to make it). So I built one place to compare them, scaled to exactly the pizzas I'm making.
