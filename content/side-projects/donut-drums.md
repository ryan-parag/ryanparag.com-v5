This project is a playful intersection of my love for beat-making and interactive web design. Inspired by the legendary producer J Dilla, Donut Drums is a browser-based, tappable drum kit. The goal was to create a low-latency, tactile experience where users could trigger soulful samples and loops, proving that the web can be a highly expressive medium for music and sound design.
