Working in distributed teams often leads to the "math tax"—constantly calculating what 10:00 AM EST looks like for a colleague in London or Poland. I built Timezones to move beyond basic world clocks, creating a highly visual interface that makes scheduling across borders intuitive. It’s a utility-first tool designed to ensure no one gets invited to a meeting at 3:00 AM their time.
