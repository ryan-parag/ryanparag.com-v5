When I first entered the design industry in Tampa, I realized there wasn't a centralized hub to find local meetups, agencies, or community events. I launched TampaBay.design to act as a digital compass for the local creative community. By aggregating resources and highlighting local talent, the site serves as a jumping-off point for designers looking to get involved and grow the design culture in the Tampa Bay area.
