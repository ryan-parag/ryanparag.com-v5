I built ScoreKeeper to solve a very specific problem: tracking scores during high-stakes lunch-break card games with my coworkers. Tired of hunting for scraps of paper and doing mental math, I designed this "stupidly-simple" web app to handle the heavy lifting. It allows players to set up custom games, manage rounds, and track leaderboards in real-time with a clean, mobile-first interface.

If you'd like to keep up with some of the onoing changes, check out the [changelog](https://scorekeeper.ryanparag.com/changelog)!
